Current capabilities and boundaries

This is a browser-based DAW foundation with multitrack audio playback, microphone recording, editable MIDI notes, a step drum machine, channel mixing, effects and WAV/MIDI export. It does not host desktop VST3/AU plugins, and it does not yet provide professional-grade automatic pitch correction or the complete feature set of FL Studio/Cubase.
